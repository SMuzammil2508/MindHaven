## 🌟 Overview

**MindHaven** is an application designed to prioritize your mental well-being. By leveraging advanced AI, it acts as a compassionate companion that offers emotional support, tracks your mood, and guides you through mindfulness exercises.

Whether you are a student facing academic pressure, a professional dealing with burnout, or simply looking for a non-judgmental space to vent, MindHaven is here to listen and help.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **📜 Mood Tracking & Journaling** Log your daily emotions and thoughts to create a personal mental health record and identify patterns over time.
* **🧘 Guided Meditation & Relaxation** Access AI-curated mindfulness exercises, breathing techniques, and relaxation sessions tailored to your current state.
* **🔒 Secure & Private** Your privacy is our priority. All journal entries and conversations are processed securely, ensuring a safe environment for self-expression.

## 👤 Target Audience

* **Students:** Managing exam stress and academic anxiety.
* **Professionals:** Coping with workplace burnout and work-life balance.
* **Mental Health Enthusiasts:** Individuals interested in mindfulness and self-growth.
* **Everyone:** Anyone seeking a supportive, private space for daily check-ins.
